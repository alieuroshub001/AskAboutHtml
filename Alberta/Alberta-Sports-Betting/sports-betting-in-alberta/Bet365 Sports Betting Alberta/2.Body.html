Is Bet365 Legal in Alberta?

Yes, Bet365 accepts players from Alberta. Alberta does not currently operate its own provincial online sports betting regulator, unlike Ontario, which is overseen by the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario (AGCO) and iGaming Ontario (iGO), so Alberta bettors access Bet365 through its internationally licensed platform.

Bet365 operates under respected international gaming licenses, including the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) and the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC), both of which enforce strict standards for fair odds, secure transactions, and responsible gambling, giving Alberta bettors a safe and well-regulated betting environment even without a made-in-Alberta license.
